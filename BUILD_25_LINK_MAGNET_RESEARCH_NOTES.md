## Public asset

- `/travel-rules-research/`
- `/downloads/canibringitnow-travel-rules-snapshot.csv`

The page is designed as a genuine citation and outreach asset for travel writers, bloggers, student travel teams and journalists. It gives outreach a stronger destination than the homepage and makes the site's structured coverage visible.
