# Growth Release 15 — Visitor Expansion

Adds a public, indexable Travel Services section designed to broaden search reach while remaining useful to travellers.

## Included
- Travel Services hub.
- Eight substantial service guides: travel insurance, airport hotels, car hire, airport parking, airport transfers, eSIM/connectivity, airport lounges, and flight disruption/compensation.
- Search-intent phrases, practical checklists, FAQs and internal links on every guide.
- FAQ structured data on service pages.
- Homepage and footer discovery links.
- High-priority sitemap treatment for the new section.

No provider endorsements or unverified price claims have been added. Affiliate partners can be integrated later without rewriting the core guidance.
